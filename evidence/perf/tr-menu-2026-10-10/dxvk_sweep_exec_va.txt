EXEC_VA bug
-----------
kbase_kmod.c set up the executable VA zone (EXEC_VA, where shader binaries
live) with 1024 pages = 4 MiB. DXVK 2.x with graphics pipeline libraries
compiles far more shader variants than 1.x and ran out of it:

  MEM_ALLOC failed: Out of memory (4 pages, flags 0x1817, executable)

thousands of times per run, ~1700 pipelines failed, the picture was broken,
and the frame rate looked higher (12-16 fps) only because most draws were
missing. The fix (0004) asks for 256 MiB, keeps the 4 MiB zone if the kernel
refuses, and PANVK_KBASE_EXEC_VA_MB overrides the size. With it every DXVK
2.x run had 0 allocation failures, 0 failed pipelines and a correct picture
(checked on screenshots).

2.3.1 MSVC build: the crash is a null read inside the game's own
msvcp140.dll (14.28), the same with and without the fix. With Wine's builtin
msvcp140 the game doesn't start. Not a driver problem.

1.12.0-sarek and 1.10.9-sarek-async: GPU load was 59-66% during the low
readings; these two need a rerun before drawing conclusions.
